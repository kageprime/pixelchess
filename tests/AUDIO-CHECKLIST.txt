Audio checklist (needs a real speaker - cannot be heard in my test environment)

1. VS CPU > pick an opponent. A YouTube track should start within a few seconds
   (needs internet - first tap unlocks autoplay):
   Mossback = Mavo - Shakabulizzy, Lady Vex = Zaylevelten - Guide Pass (prod. Tenski),
   Old Dune = Tochi Bedford - impossible.
   A small hidden player streams the audio (no video box on screen); only the
   credit line shows bottom-right.
   The old chiptune loop plays first, then fades out once the YouTube track is audible.
   Offline / embed blocked: chiptune keeps playing as fallback (same tunes as before).
2. Move a piece. The thud should match the world: wood clack (garden), stone tick (hall), soft sand (desert).
3. Put a king in check (or lose 3+ points of material). A pulsing arpeggio and low throb should fade in over a few moves,
   then fade out when things calm down.
4. Play a long game: Mossback gets crickets once the sky turns to night; Lady Vex has torch crackle; Old Dune has wind gusts.
5. Pause: music should duck to a murmur and come back on Resume (both chiptune and YouTube).
6. SND OFF (menu or pause) silences everything, including the YouTube player;
   SND ON during a game brings the world track back.
7. Checkmate: the music stops (YouTube pauses too) and the win or lose sting plays on the result screen.
8. Quit to menu or 2 PLAYERS: no music (2-player mode has no scene).

If anything is too loud or too quiet, the levels are the numbers in the MUS table (lv = lead volume) and bus gain .7 in musStart.

New in phase 4: a short two-note blip plays when a piece hops after capturing a knight, bishop, rook or queen,
and the mate sequence plays a crash and thud when the king lands. Check that they feel in proportion.
