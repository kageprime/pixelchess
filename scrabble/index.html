HOW TO PLAY

TAP A RACK TILE, THEN TAP A SQUARE. TAP A PLACED TILE TO TAKE IT BACK.

PLAY IN ONE ROW OR COLUMN. THE FIRST WORD COVERS THE STAR. LATER WORDS MUST TOUCH TILES ALREADY ON THE BOARD.

COLOURED SQUARES BOOST ONLY THE TILES YOU PLACE. USING ALL 7 TILES SCORES 50 EXTRA.

PASS THE PHONE AFTER EACH TURN. LOAD A WORD LIST FROM THE MENU TO CHECK WORDS.
